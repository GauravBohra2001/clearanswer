# Sample claims from the Nature 2012 TCGA-BRCA paper

Four verbatim-shaped sentences that mirror claims made in
*Comprehensive molecular portraits of human breast tumours*
(Nature, 2012; doi:10.1038/nature11412). Paste this file into ClearAnswer to
see the plug-and-play semantic layer in action.

These are checkable against the current TCGA-BRCA release pulled by
`data/tcga-brca/fetch.py`. The paper used a 2011 freeze, so numbers may
have drifted.

---

Tumour and germline DNA samples were obtained from 825 patients.

TP53 mutations occurred in 80% of basal-like cases.

The most frequent being PIK3CA (45%) in Luminal A subtype.

348 of the 466 samples also had reverse-phase protein array data.
